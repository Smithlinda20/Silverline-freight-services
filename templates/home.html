Important Notice Regarding Delivery Charges

Please be informed that certain processing charges are applicable during the shipment and delivery of your package. These charges include:

  1. Security and Safety Fee - $150 (Refundable)
  2. Clearance Procedures Fee - $210 (Refundable)
  3. Regulatory Agency Processing Fee - $320 (Refundable)
  4. End-of-Line Processing Fee - $500 (Refundable)
  5. Final Inspection Fee - $600 (Refundable)
  6. Final Handling Fee - $700

These fees are temporary security deposits required to ensure that all regulatory, safety, and compliance procedures are properly completed throughout the delivery process.

Upon successful delivery of the package, all refundable charges will be returned in full directly to the recipient by the courier at the point of delivery.

Please note that delivery cannot be completed unless all required procedures and associated charges are fully settled.

For assistance, contact our delivery agents: +1 (559) 889-6546 and +1 (602) 654-5189.
